Privacy Policy

Current Revision: May 2026

1. Privacy Statement & Governance

At D&C Standards, data confidentiality and digital infrastructure security are core operating parameters. This Privacy Policy delineates how we harvest, store, protect, and process credentials provided by users, in alignment with Colombian Statute 1581 of 2012 (General Regime of Personal Data Protection) and executive Decree 1377 of 2013.

2. Information We Harvest

Through our digital touchpoints (web log forms, WhatsApp chat routers, or corporate mail servers), we capture strictly professional, business-to-business corporate data metrics:

  • Full identity name of the partner representative or technical operator contact.
  • Registered legal corporate title of the contracting firm entity.
  • Official corporate business email channel destination.
  • Telephone cellular contact or operational office line digits.
  • Technical data files or schematic project specs attached to a bidding quote request.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

Data harvesting operates solely for legitimate industrial, engineering management, and corporate commerce workflows:

  • Drafting, transmitting, and analyzing financial-technical engineering bids for heavy manufacture or API certification assets.
  • Structuring responsive engineering project update communication lines.
  • Contract account management, manufacturing invoicing files, and asset history logging.
  • Broadcasting notifications on asset certifications, quality system audits, or facility expansions.

4. Confidentiality & Third-Party Non-Disclosure

D&C Standards certifies that corporate details and technical project blueprints (calculation books, well criteria logs, structural layout blocks) shall never be distributed, sold, or leased to third parties for merchant gains. Blueprints or engineering logs are shared with NDT testing labs or inspection authorities only when strictly required for equipment recertification and with prior client approval.

5. Data Subject Rights (Habeas Data)

Under governing data rules, you hold full authority to review, update, rectify, or demand the absolute erasure of your personal details from our records at any point. To file a claim, submit an explicit request notice to our compliance board via info@dcstandards.com.

6. Security Infrastructure

We deploy robust digital encryptions and network clearance levels to shield stored technical and corporate records from threat access breaches, structural loss, or layout modifications.
