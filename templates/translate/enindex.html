01 / WHO WE ARE

ENGINEERING SOLUTIONS FOR THE OILFIELD SECTOR.

We are a premier firm specialized in corporate structural engineering designs and heavy metalworking manufacturing operations tailored for the global Oil & Gas upstream industries. Operating directly within Colombia, our production assets deploy engineering criteria aligned under strict compliance with the API 4F normative for manufacturing heavy-duty masts, drilling substructures, and surface control assets. Our commitment to excellence, backed by internal non-destructive testing and a highly qualified workforce asset, guarantees robust operational uptime and safety.

SERVICES

01 / API CERTIFICATION

CATEGORIES III AND IV

We perform comprehensive structural inspections in accordance with the API RP 4G standard. We conduct Category III (detailed visual) and Category IV (major disassembly and NDT testing) maintenance to recertify the integrity of masts and substructures.

03 / OPTIMIZATION

UPGRADES

Comprehensive asset modernization to increase load capacity (Hook Load) and operational efficiency. We upgrade substructures, masts, and control systems by integrating state-of-the-art technology without the need to purchase new equipment.

03 / OPERATIONS

DESIGN

We execute conceptual, basic, and high-detail structural design engineering under API standards. Advanced 3D modeling, dynamic stress calculations, and Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to secure complete structural integrity.

04 / TECHNOLOGY

MANUFACTURING

Heavy steel fabrication layouts using premium high-strength alloys, custom multi-process certified welding, and systematic Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) criteria tailored for aggressive operational environments.

05 / LOGISTICS

MAINTENANCE

Complete structural recertification protocols, critical weld path inspections, engineering component retrofitting, and specialized on-site field assembly logistics to secure continuous field asset performance.
